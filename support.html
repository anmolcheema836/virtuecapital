How might I track the progress of my goods?

+

Upon the dispatch of your wares, a rider is sent forth bearing an electronic missive. Within this message, you will find a unique cypher. Present this cypher to the website of the designated courier to receive timely updates on your parcel's journey.

What are the established means of conveyance?

+

We offer Standard Conveyance via reliable wagon train (5-7 days) and Expedited Dispatch via the Pony Express (2-3 days). All shipments originate from our primary depot in Philadelphia.

What is the protocol for returning unsatisfactory items?

+

Should you find an article not to your satisfaction, you are afforded a period of thirty days to return it for a full reimbursement of shillings. The item must be returned in the condition it was received, with its original packaging intact.
