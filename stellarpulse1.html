About Stellarpulse & Contact

Stellarpulse is a non-profit collective dedicated to bringing high-energy, progressive electronic music to Switzerland. We believe in fostering a vibrant community through meticulously curated events that blend futuristic aesthetics with raw, underground sounds. Our mission is to create unforgettable experiences that push the boundaries of sound and vision.
