Objective of the Competition

Create a first draft on each of the following media:

  • DTP / graphic design (file, booklet, layout – Adobe InDesign, Illustrator, Photoshop)
  • Presentation video (Adobe Premiere and/or After Effects)
  • Website (WordPress, with SEO databases)

This first phase will be used to assess understanding of the brief, graphic quality and creative consistency. The successful freelancer will then be able to continue the full mission (final development of each media).

Requirements

  • Respect the formats and structure of the provided template
  • Keep all drafts, tests and intermediate versions (necessary for the training booklet and the oral presentation)
  • Explain the creative process and graphic choices
  • Use exclusively Adobe software for DTP and video

Budget & Continuation of the Project

An initial fee will be allocated for this draft phase. Freelancers offering coherent and aesthetic work will be prioritized for the complete continuation of the project (finalization of the file, video and site).

What I expect for your participation

  • A visual preview or a mock-up of intent on one or more media (even partial)
  • A budget and deadline proposal for this first phase
  • And if possible, some references of previous work in the field of graphic design or visual communication

Thank you in advance to all participants for their creativity and seriousness!
