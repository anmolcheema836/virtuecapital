About PEI Food Strong, Inc.

Our Mission: To future-proof Prince Edward Island's food security by empowering home cooks, supporting local producers, and educating the next generation.

All proceeds from our cookbooks go towards community workshops, school garden initiatives, and supporting local food banks.
