Art Deco Styling

Since 1946
A Proper Institution.

Opened in 1946 on Regency Street, near Westminster. We are defined by our Art Deco tiling, original interior, and the photographs of Tottenham Hotspur players that line the walls.

The tables are Formica-topped, the orders are shouted loudly, and the tea is hot. No fuss. Just honest food in a place that feels like a movie set—because it often is one.
