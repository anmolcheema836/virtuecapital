Frequently Asked Questions

Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. We are fully licensed and insured. This gives clients added confidence that they are working with a professional construction and development team.
What does Certified Lean Six Sigma mean for my project?
Lean Six Sigma focuses on improving processes, reducing waste, and creating efficient workflows. In construction, this supports better planning, stronger coordination, clearer communication, and improved project control.
Do you handle both residential and commercial construction?
Yes. BULADEV and ASA Construction LLC support both residential and commercial construction projects, including new builds, renovations, tenant build-outs, and property upgrades.
Can you help with land development?
Yes. BULADEV provides land development support, including project planning, site preparation coordination, property improvement strategy, and construction-readiness support.
How do I start a project?
The first step is to contact us by phone, email, or website form. We will discuss your project goals, property details, timeline, and define the next steps.
