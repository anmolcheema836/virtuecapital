Welcome

A Cozy Corner in the West End

Our aim was to create a warm and cosy environment in the city centre, serving up traditional Scottish breakfasts, healthy bowls, and bringing a small taste of Poland to Scotland!

All our dishes are prepared fresh and only with the best quality local ingredients. We do not use pre-prepared products at all so everything you receive on your plate is made especially for you, with our love.

Trust us... it will be worth the wait.
