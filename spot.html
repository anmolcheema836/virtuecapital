End-to-End Remodelling

From first consult to final walkthrough, we manage the full renovation process with premium materials, tidy sites and reliable timelines.

We perform demolition by placing sufficient skip bins to declutter broken fittings and debris, making the space ready for renovation.

Certified tradesmen carry out all plumbing works and electrical services including lighting, power points, and switchboard upgrades to Australian standards.

We apply waterproof membrane to shower walls and floors (AS 3740 compliance), followed by precision tiling and grouting for a flawless, water-resistant finish.

Installation of all bathroom fixtures—shower screens, vanities, bathtubs, and toilets—ensuring perfect alignment and sealing.
