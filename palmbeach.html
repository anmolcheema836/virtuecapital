About Us

Continuing a Legacy of Fine Craftsmanship

The Dicembrino family has a heritage in design and upholstery. Originally from Palermo Sicily, our aunt was a clothing designer in NY and our uncle specializes in custom furniture and window treatments for Palm Beach Island.

We are proud to be continuing their legacy of fine craftsmanship. We bring the showroom to your home offering a wide variety of quality products from local, national and worldwide manufacturers with expert installation.

We provide everything from custom roller, solar, banded, roman shades, blinds, Plantation shutters to beautiful drapes and panels. We look forward to working with you to add the finishing touches your home or office deserves.
