( About The Studio )

We elevate the essence of interior architecture through uncompromising bespoke millwork and timeless craftsmanship.

Founded on the principle that true luxury lies in the details unseen, Atelier has been the silent partner behind award-winning residences and commercial spaces for over two decades. Our workshop bridges the gap between old-world joinery techniques and cutting-edge precision engineering.

Every piece of timber that enters our studio is hand-selected. We believe that wood is a living medium, and our craft is simply to guide it into its most beautiful, functional form. From sweeping paneled libraries to minimalist kitchen architecture, our work stands as a testament to patience and skill.

"Woodworking is not merely about shaping timber; it is the profound art of shaping the atmosphere of a room."

— Julian Vance, Master Carpenter

Our Process

01 — 04
01.

Vision & Consultation

We collaborate closely with architects, designers, and private clients to understand the spatial requirements, aesthetic goals, and functional demands of the interior.

02.

Design & Engineering

Our technical drafting team translates concepts into exact shop drawings. We source rare veneers and solid timbers, mapping out grain matching and structural integrity.

03.

Master Craftsmanship

Fabrication takes place in our state-of-the-art facility. Here, traditional joinery meets modern machinery, ensuring every joint, edge, and finish is executed flawlessly.

04.

Flawless Installation

Our dedicated installation team ensures that the transition from workshop to site is seamless, handling the final fitting with white-glove precision and care.

F.A.Q.

Answers to the most common questions regarding our bespoke carpentry services, materials, and operations.

Do you work internationally?

+

Yes. While our workshop is based domestically, we regularly engineer, crate, and ship bespoke millwork for luxury residences and boutiques worldwide. Our installation masters travel globally to ensure our standards are met on site.

What is the typical timeline for a custom project?

+

Timelines vary significantly based on scale and material rarity. A single bespoke piece may take 6-8 weeks, whereas a full architectural millwork package for a residence generally requires 4-6 months from final shop drawing approval to installation.

Do you collaborate directly with interior designers?

+

Absolutely. The majority of our work is done in close partnership with high-end interior design firms and architects. We act as their technical arm, translating their aesthetic vision into engineered, buildable reality.

What sustainably sourced materials do you use?

+

We prioritize FSC-certified lumbers and ethical veneers. Our network of suppliers allows us to trace the origin of our hardwoods, ensuring environmental responsibility without compromising on the luxurious finishes our clients expect.
