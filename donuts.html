About Yonni's

Yonnis Bakery is part of the Discovery Coffee family. Every morning our incredible bakery team prepares the glaze, doughs, toppings and fillings by hand, and from scratch in our bakery on Discovery Street.

We use quality ingredients, and our own original, tried-and-tested recipes to craft what we think are the BEST line-up of hand crafted doughnuts you will taste! Our doughnuts are always made from scratch, never from a mix, and we NEVER use artificial flavours or mystery ingredients.

Our doughnuts and treats are fried or baked, and delivered fresh every morning to our Discovery Coffee cafe locations and select wholesale accounts across Victoria.

On each visit you'll find the classics – old fashioneds, fritters, and crullers, and classic vanilla glazed rings. We also prepare exciting monthly features, special holiday treats, as well as a growing number of vegan treats.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I order doughnuts for today? +
Online orders are for next day and later. The deadline for next day orders is 12pm (noon). If you would like fresh doughnuts today, please go to one of our 4 Discovery Coffee cafe locations.
When are you open? +
We are open 7 days a week, frying fresh doughnuts every morning!
What is the minimum order? +
Our minimum online order is 6 doughnuts.
Do you use a mix for your doughnuts? +
ALL our doughnuts are made from scratch, never a mix, and they are cut and formed by hand. Heck we're SO old school we use a rolling pin to roll out our dough.
Do you have vegan options? +
Yes, every day we make our Classic Rings and our Maple Apple fritters, which are always vegan. We also offer a Vegan Mixed Dozen box.
