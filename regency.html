Heritage

A Proper London Café

Opened in 1946 on Regency Street, we have stood as a beacon of traditional British dining for nearly 80 years. Step past our original black tiling and into a piece of Westminster history.

Voted the 5th best eating place in London (Yelp, 2013), we are known for our loud service, queueing crowds, and formica-topped tables. Whether you are a local politician or a visiting film fan, you get the same treatment: honest food, served hot.
