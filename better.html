ABOUT THE AUTHOR

SAMMY HAMMONS

Sammy Hammons is a faith-driven entrepreneur, author, and founder of the 1% Better Movement, dedicated to helping men grow stronger in body, mind, and spirit—one step at a time.

With over twenty years of experience in business, leadership, and personal development, Sammy blends practical wisdom with biblical truth to inspire men to take consistent action toward their God-given potential.

Through his Masculine Revival Playbook community and his book One Percent Better Every Day, Sammy challenges men to stop chasing perfection and start building discipline, strength, and purpose—one small win at a time.

Sammy enjoys riding motorcycles, golfing, and spending time with his family.
