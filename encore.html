Let Me Introduce Myself

A Personal Journey of Inspiration and Purpose

Bill Leighty’s journey into The Encore Project was ignited by a profound realization—an awakening of both personal and professional significance. After 35 years of dedicated service in the real estate industry, he yearned for more than just financial and emotional fulfillment.

Driven by an unwavering commitment to provide for his family and maintain their stability, Bill embarked on a deliberate process of self-reflection and exploration, seeking answers to life’s timeless questions.

Drawing from in-depth market research and a profound understanding of the needs of senior men, The Encore Project seeks to create an immersive digital experience. Through a dynamic blend of captivating articles, compelling videos, thought-provoking podcasts, and interactive content.
