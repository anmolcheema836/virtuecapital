Our Team

Lucy Millard

Partner

Lucy has practiced primarily in the field of child and family law since qualifying as a solicitor in 2011. She adopts a pragmatic approach to resolve difficult situations as efficiently and effectively as possible. Lucy is appointed regularly to act in an independent role to assist the Court in child cases, as Reporter and Curator ad litem in residence and contact disputes. Lucy is often instructed to represent other solicitors appointed as curators in contentious court proceedings. Lucy has significant experience in adoption and permanence order proceedings... Lucy is accredited by the Law Society of Scotland as a specialist in Child Law.
