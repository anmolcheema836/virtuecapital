Our Story

Welcome

"Like an escape to a cosy 'grandma's style' living room."

Café Bella Maria is an independent, Italian and English family run café located on Lower Grosvenor Place, just a stone’s throw from Buckingham Palace.

We pride ourselves on our 'everything from scratch' attitude. All our food is completely home-made, from our delicious cakes right down to our pesto. Enjoy your tea in gilt-rimmed mismatch china cups amongst picture filled walls and an old playable piano.
