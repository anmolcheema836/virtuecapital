Our Mission

Stellarpulse is a non-profit collective dedicated to curating powerful, immersive electronic music experiences in Switzerland. We unite diverse subcultures under a canopy of stars and sound, fusing cosmic visuals with cutting-edge techno and trance. Our events are safe spaces for expression, connection, and interstellar journeys.
