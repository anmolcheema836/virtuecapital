Frequently Asked Questions

What wholesaler accounts do I get access to? ▼

You get access to systems like TradeZone, Ideal Electrical, and Voltex, utilizing our aggregated purchasing volumes.

How does Affiliate Warehouse make money? ▼

We charge a transparent 1.1% fee only on verified discounts. If you don't save money, we don't charge you.

Am I locked in? ▼

No. There are no contracts, no minimum spend, and no exclusivity. You can stop using the service at any time.

Is this compliant with supplier systems? ▼

Yes. We use the same backend access and traceability. Purchases are processed through normal wholesaler channels—no reselling or bypassing.

How are better prices achieved? ▼

Better pricing is achieved through aggregated purchasing volume across our network of independent preferred contractors nationally.
