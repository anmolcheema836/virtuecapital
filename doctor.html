About Dr. Don Utas

Devoted to helping people through modern, compassionate dentistry.

Dr. Don Utas received his Dental degree from the University of Saskatchewan in 1994 and bought Meadowbrook Dental in Chilliwack a year later. As a lifelong learner, he has earned a Diploma of Technology at BCIT and a Bachelor of Education at Simon Fraser University.

Before devoting his life to dentistry, Dr. Utas worked as a Park Ranger and high school teacher. These diverse experiences have shaped his approach to patient care, allowing him to explain complex dental health concepts with clarity and patience.

Laser Dentistry Specialist

Since 2006, Dr. Utas has been committed to offering the most up-to-date technology to his patients. He received Mastership Certification in Laser Dentistry at the World Clinical Laser Institute in California and continues to learn about new procedures through continuing education in the US and Canada.

Laser dentistry is a safe and effective alternative to the dental drill. It reduces or eliminates the need for freezing in many cases.

Located in Chilliwack, Meadowbrook Dental specializes in the use of lasers to treat a wide range of dental problems. Dr. Utas and his team are dedicated to providing leading-edge laser dentistry to residents of the Fraser Valley in a comfortable, welcoming environment.
