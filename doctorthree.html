Dr. Don Utas

Dentist & Owner | Since 1995
"I have fun with what I do. Lasers allow me to do more for my patients and, in my opinion, are a better way than using the old-fashioned methods."

A Passion for Dentistry

Dr. Don Utas received his Dental degree from the University of Saskatchewan in 1994 and bought Meadowbrook Dental in Chilliwack a year later. His journey to dentistry was unique; he worked as a Park Ranger and high school teacher before devoting his life to helping people improve their oral health.

As a lifelong learner, Dr. Utas has earned a Diploma of Technology at BCIT and a Bachelor of Education at Simon Fraser University. This educational background allows him to explain complex procedures to his patients with clarity and care.

Expertise in Laser Dentistry

Since 2006, Dr. Utas has been committed to offering the most up-to-date technology. He received Mastership Certification in Laser Dentistry at the World Clinical Laser Institute. Lasers allow him to remove decay and prepare teeth for fillings painlessly, often reducing or eliminating the need for freezing.
