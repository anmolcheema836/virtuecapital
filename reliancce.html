Who We Are

Dedicated to Enhancing Independence & Well-being.

At Reliance Care Solutions, we believe that everyone deserves the highest quality of care in a safe, nurturing environment. Whether you are seeking temporary relief through our respite programs or comprehensive developmental support, our highly trained direct care workers are here.

New Programs

Youth & Children Services

Targeted interventions, structured environments, and certified assessments ensuring every child's unique needs are met with empathy and expertise.

Certified CANS Assessments

We conduct certified assessments for children using the Child and Adolescent Needs and Strengths (CANS) tool. This clinical process ensures that each child's unique emotional and behavioral needs are accurately identified and addressed promptly.

Group Home Services

Offering a safe, supportive living environment for youth. Our group homes focus heavily on individual needs, personal growth, life skills development, and overall well-being within a highly structured and caring setting.

Emergency Respite Placement

Providing critical, immediate respite placement for children facing crises. Our rapid-response program guarantees a secure, nurturing environment to stabilize situations and offer immediate support when families need it most.

Specialized Care & Training

Horse & Animal Interactions

Experience the profound therapeutic benefits of animal interaction. These specialized activities actively promote emotional healing, reduce anxiety, and enhance personal development in a highly safe, supervised, and nurturing environment.

On-Call Nurse Services

Immediate, professional medical support whenever required. Our fully trained clinical staff is continuously available to address any medical concerns or emergencies promptly, efficiently, and with the utmost care.

Expert Staff Training (CPI & PFS)

Safety is paramount. All staff members are rigorously trained in Crisis Prevention Institute (CPI) techniques to safely de-escalate and manage challenging situations. Our team also holds Prevention First (PFS) certifications to deliver immediate, critical care.
