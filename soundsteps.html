A calm space for everyday progress

SoundSteps breaks down practice into simple, structured moments that fit naturally into your day. No pressure, just positive reinforcement.

1. Listen to a Sound

Hear clear, targeted speech sounds in a distraction-free environment, demonstrating how the sound is formed.

2. Child Repeats

Your child is given a gentle, unpressured moment to try repeating the sound, building their confidence step by step.

3. Parent Encourages

You lead the session. The app guides you on how to offer positive reinforcement and gentle corrections without causing frustration.

4. Save & Track Progress

Recordings and milestones are saved securely, giving you a timeline of progress and providing insights you can eventually share with a professional.
