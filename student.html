10 Words or Less Scholarship

Value $1,000
Awards 2
Deadline April 15, 2026

The 10 Words or Less Scholarship is provided by StudentScholarships.org. StudentScholarships.org has been providing scholarship information to students and scholarship management services to companies since 2003. It is considered the largest database for high school students and college students in the country.

To be eligible for the 10 Words or Less Scholarship a student must:

  • A) Be between the ages of 14-25 who will be attending school in the Fall of 2026.
  • B) Be attending a school in the US or Canada in the Fall of 2026.
  • C) Be a high school senior or graduate, or a current college undergraduate.

If selected as a recipient of the 10 Words or Less Scholarship, one student will receive a $1,000 award and one student will receive a $500 award.

Awards are not renewable.

Awards are for qualified education-related expenses only: tuition, fees, books, and required supplies (including laptops).

The 10 Words or Less Scholarship judges will evaluate applicants based on:

  • Response to Question

Note that you must plan on attending school in the next academic year. You can be enrolled in any level of post-secondary studies.

Scholarship winners will be posted on this website by May 15, 2026. Winners will be contacted directly. If you are not selected, you may return to this page on or after May 15, 2026.

What is the value of the Award?

For the April 15, 2026 deadline, the 10 Words or Less Scholarship will provide one student with a $1,000 award and one student with a $500 award.
