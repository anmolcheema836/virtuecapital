The Friction

Fleet maintenance can be remarkably complicated.

Moving large commercial vehicles to distant repair shops disrupts logistics, strains schedules, and obscures visibility. Historically, fleet maintenance has been a loud, reactive, and fragmented process. We believe billions of dollars of transportation infrastructure deserve a quieter, more highly engineered approach.

The FleetSquad Standard
