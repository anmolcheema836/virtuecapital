What You Need To Know

What is a Dental Implant?

Unlike traditional removable dentures, dental implants provide a permanent and secure alternative for tooth replacement. An implant itself is a titanium screw that’s carefully placed into your jawbone. Over time, your bone fuses with the implant, creating a strong foundation that acts just like a natural tooth root.

Once healed, the implants can support teeth. Multiple implants would be required for a full bridge. Many of our patients in Aberdeenshire and surrounding areas have regained their smiles through our expertly carried out dental implant treatment right here in Turriff.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants are the closest thing to having your own natural teeth again. They stay fixed in place, so you can eat, speak, and smile freely - without worrying about slipping dentures.

  • A natural look and feel that matches your real teeth
  • Long-lasting results that can last decades
  • Easy daily maintenance, just like regular teeth
  • Protection for your bone health and facial structure
  • Improved confidence and a beautifully natural smile

Frequently Asked Questions

Unlike traditional removable dentures, dental implants provide a permanent and secure alternative for tooth replacement. An implant itself is a titanium screw implant that’s carefully placed into your jawbone. Over time, your bone fuses with the implant, creating a strong foundation that acts just like a natural tooth root.

A small titanium screw implant is placed into the bone beneath your missing tooth. The gum is gently secured over it, allowing the bone to integrate with the implant. Finally, a beautiful custom-made replacement tooth is attached.

Depending on your needs, we offer implants for single-tooth replacement, replacing multiple teeth, Full Arch dental implants, and Overdenture implants. We use Straumann dental implants, renowned worldwide.

At Turriff Dental Care, the replacement teeth cost starts from approximately £2500 per implant. Prices may vary depending on your individual treatment plan and whether additional work - like bone grafting - is needed.
