The Kitchen

Fresh. Local. Wild.

At Kraken Seafood Kitchen, we believe seafood is best served simply and generously. Located in the heart of Canningvale, we source the freshest catches—from local Barramundi to Red Emperor.

Whether you crave the crunch of our golden batter, the zest of our Greek Lemon dressing, or the heat of our Spicy Sambal, every dish is crafted to honor the produce.
