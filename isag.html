Operational Threat

What Happens to Most
Field Intelligence?

Every law enforcement agency collects valuable field intelligence each day, yet most of it becomes lost, overlooked, or underutilized.

"This breakdown places officers at risk, allows criminal activity to continue unchecked, and weakens investigations."

Information is captured informally, buried in disconnected systems, or never analyzed to support real-time operations. This critical data decay is the primary obstacle to modern policing.

The TIU Intervention

A Tactical Intelligence Unit (TIU) solves this problem by creating a structured process to capture, centralize, analyze, and distribute field intelligence. Designed for small- and mid-sized departments, the TIU transforms disconnected observations into actionable intelligence.
