Our Expertise

Comprehensive Financial Services

From compliance to strategic insights, we cover the four pillars of business finance.

Accounting

  • Financial: Year-end reconciliations, Fixed Asset Register, Depreciation.
  • Managerial: Budgeting, Forecasting, Variance Analysis.
  • Profitability monitoring & KPI variance reports.
  • Benchmarking against industry competitors.

Bookkeeping

  • Transactional: AP/AR, Bank & Credit Card Reconciliation.
  • Setup of new accounts & Chart of Accounts review.
  • Integration with Xero add-ons (Dext, Stripe, etc).
  • Internal control & document management.

Payroll

  • Processing payroll & adjusting payments.
  • Calculating Annual Leave & Long Service Leave.
  • Superannuation accruals & clearing house interaction.
  • Reviewing and modifying existing payroll setups.

Compliance

  • ATO: BAS, IAS, GST Annual Return, STP, TPAR.
  • State: Payroll tax (e.g. Revenue NSW).
  • Insurance: Workers Comp, Public Liability audit prep.
  • Industry-specific body requirements (e.g. Master Builders).

Note: We do not provide Tax Returns or stand-alone BAS lodgements without prior bookkeeping.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide Tax Returns?
No, we do not provide Tax Return services or tax planning advice. We focus on Bookkeeping, Accounting, Payroll, and operational compliance.
Can you do stand-alone BAS lodgements?
We do not provide stand-alone BAS lodgements. We believe accurate lodgement requires us to handle the prior bookkeeping to ensure data integrity.
Which software do you use?
We are experienced with Xero, MYOB, QuickBooks Online, and various add-ons like Dext, PipeDrive, and Stripe.
Do you work with businesses outside Sydney?
Yes, we provide services to small and medium-sized businesses all over Australia using cloud-based technologies.
