Curriculum.

Structured pathways from the fundamental opening principles to elite endgame calculation.

01 The Foundation +

Designed for the uninitiated. Master board geometry, piece coordination, absolute and relative pins, and the mathematical value of position over material.

02 Tactical Warfare +

Intermediate module focusing strictly on pattern recognition. Sacrifices, discovered attacks, deflections, and building the calculating stamina required for long tournament play.

03 Positional Mastery +

Advanced concepts pioneered by Karpov and Petrosian. Prophylaxis, weak square exploitation, pawn structure integrity, and suffocating your opponent through microscopic advantages.

04 Grandmaster Preparation +

Elite tier. Deep engine analysis, bespoke opening repertoire creation, psychological resilience, and mastery of theoretical endgames. Taught exclusively by titled GMs.
