About Stellarpulse

Stellarpulse is a non-profit collective born from a passion for boundary-pushing electronic music. We're dedicated to curating immersive experiences, bringing together progressive, psy-trance, hitech, and hard-techno artists to Switzerland's vibrant underground scene. Our mission is to foster community, elevate sonic exploration, and ignite the dance floor with unparalleled energy.
