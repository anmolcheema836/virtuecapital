Frequently asked questions

Is Commenter AI just an AI commenting tool? ▼

No.

Commenter AI is not built to automate commenting for volume. It’s built to connect your comments and DMs to sales signals, so you can see what matters and what doesn’t.

AI plays a supporting role: drafts comments and replies faster, learns your writing style over time, and can use your own insights. You stay in control of what’s sent and how you engage.

Who is Commenter AI best suited for? ▼

Commenter AI works best for people who already use LinkedIn to drive conversations and want more focus, not more activity.

  • B2B founders (solo or with small teams)
  • Consultants and service providers
  • Small sales teams using LinkedIn for conversations and deals

Typically, they have a proven offer ($1k+), have made sales through LinkedIn, and want clarity on what to follow up. It helps scale what already works.

Do I need a credit card to start the free trial? ▼

No.

You can start the 7-day trial without a credit card. If you choose to keep using Commenter AI after the trial, you can pick a plan then.

What should I expect in my first 30 days? ▼

The first month is about visibility and focus, not instant outcomes.

  • Week 1: comments and DMs visible in one place
  • Weeks 2-3: clearer signals on which conversations are worth following up
  • By Day 30: patterns emerge around what leads to momentum

Commenter AI is built to deliver clarity first. Outcomes are built on clarity.

Does Commenter AI guarantee leads or deals? ▼

No.

Results depend on your offer, your conversations, skills, and how you follow up. Commenter AI doesn’t promise deals. It helps you see what moves deals forward so you can act with confidence.

What if I’m not seeing clarity yet? ▼

That’s normal early on.

Commenter AI doesn’t force outcomes, it reveals patterns as conversations happen. If you’re not seeing clarity, it usually means there isn't enough interaction data yet, or conversations haven't progressed far enough to show signals.

If you feel unsure during your trial, you can reach out and we’ll help you understand what you’re seeing.

Do I need to post content regularly for this to work? ▼

No.

Commenter AI works whether you post often, comment strategically, or focus mainly on DMs. It’s designed around interaction, not posting frequency.

Is my LinkedIn account safe? ▼

Yes.

Commenter AI connects securely and works within normal LinkedIn usage patterns. You stay in control of what’s sent and when.

Can I cancel anytime? ▼

Yes.

There are no contracts. You can cancel anytime directly from your account.
