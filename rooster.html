Who We Are

Warm & Cosy.

Everything on your plate is made especially for you, from scratch, with our love.

We do not use pre-prepared products at all. Whilst we do try to work as quickly as possible, sometimes our dishes take a little longer than usual at busier times. The quality of the food we serve is most important to us, so we thank you for your understanding.

We are a small team with limited resources. We aim to reduce waste with most of the items on our menu being completely customisable.

Dietary Requirements & Allergens

Please inform a member of staff before ordering and we will try our best to accommodate.
