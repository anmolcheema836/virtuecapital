Autumn, the Designer

With a background in architecture and a lifelong passion for horticulture, Autumn founded Summer Breeze to create thoughtful, understated gardens that stand the test of time. Her design philosophy is rooted in the belief that a garden should be a natural extension of the home—a sanctuary that reflects the client's lifestyle and the surrounding environment.

Our work is inspired by the calm and tactile restraint of contemporary architecture, focusing on texture, sustainability, and thoughtful design to create a seamless flow between inside and out.
