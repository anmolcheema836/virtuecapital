STRONG ENOUGH TO BREAK

A 31-Day Devotional Guide to Understanding and Processing Grief

By Tony Ray Smith

Get Strong Enough to Break Now

GRIEF CHANGES EVERYTHING!

It changes how you think, how you feel, how you pray, and how you move through ordinary days. You may look strong on the outside while feeling overwhelmed inside. You may be carrying losses you never fully processed. You may be tired of pretending you’re okay.

Strong Enough to Break is a 31-day devotional designed to help you slow down, face your grief honestly, and begin healing with practical guidance rooted in faith, compassion, and real-life experience.

This is not about “getting over” grief. It’s about learning how to move through it — one day at a time.

Imagine what could change in 31 days

What would happen if you gave yourself permission to feel, reflect, and heal — just a little each day? What if breaking was not failure, but the beginning of restoration?

What you’ll discover inside

  • How unprocessed grief shows up in everyday life
  • Why strength and vulnerability can exist together
  • How to recognize emotional triggers and dry wells
  • The role forgiveness plays in healing
  • How faith supports the grief journey without denying the pain
  • Steps to move from survival toward restoration

This devotional is for you if:

  • You’ve experienced loss and feel stuck
  • You look strong but feel exhausted inside
  • You want faith-based guidance that feels practical and honest
  • You’re ready to process grief instead of avoiding it
About the Author

Tony Ray Smith

Tony Ray Smith is an author, speaker, pastor, and Certified Grief Recovery Method Specialist who has guided many individuals through the grief recovery process since 2014.

Through years of ministry, counseling, and practical grief work, he has helped people find language for their pain and tools for genuine healing. His approach combines compassionate faith-based insight with proven recovery principles that create space for honest emotional growth.
