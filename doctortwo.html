Meet The Team

Dr. Don Utas

A lifelong learner and educator devoted to helping people through compassionate, modern dentistry.

Dr. Don Utas received his Dental degree from the University of Saskatchewan in 1994 and bought Meadowbrook Dental in Chilliwack a year later. His path to dentistry was paved with diverse experiences; before devoting his life to oral health, he worked as a Park Ranger and high school teacher. These roles instilled in him a patience and educational approach that he brings to every patient interaction.

Dr. Utas continues to expand his knowledge through a Bachelor of Education at Simon Fraser University and a Diploma of Technology at BCIT. He is committed to offering the most up-to-date technology to his patients, ensuring treatments are as comfortable and effective as possible.

Specialization: Laser Dentistry

Since 2006, Dr. Utas has specialized in laser dentistry. This leading-edge technology is a safe and effective alternative to the dental drill.

  • Removes decay and prepares teeth painlessly.
  • Reduces or eliminates the need for freezing.
  • Treats a wide range of dental problems comfortably.

Meadowbrook Dental Laser Clinic provides leading-edge laser dentistry to residents of the Fraser Valley.
