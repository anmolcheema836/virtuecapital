Frequently Asked Questions

How does online vehicle deregistration work?

Simply enter your vehicle data and scratched-off security codes into our portal. We securely transmit this data to the central vehicle registry to complete the deregistration immediately.

Which information is required?

You need your license plate number, VIN, the hidden security code on your Registration Certificate (Part I), and the hidden codes under the seals of your license plates.

How long does processing take?

The online submission takes just a few minutes. Once submitted, the central registry usually processes the deregistration in real-time or within 24 hours.

What does the service cost?

Our comprehensive service, including data transmission and processing fees, costs a flat rate of €29.95.

Is the service available throughout Germany?

Yes, our portal is connected to the central systems and supports vehicles registered in any district across Germany.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. We use SSL encryption and comply fully with German GDPR (DSGVO) regulations to ensure your data is safe and processed solely for deregistration.
